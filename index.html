The problem

A library the batch actually keeps.

Nobody plans for the group chat to become the archive. It just happens, and then the file is forty messages back.

Every batch builds the same thing by accident. A handout gets photographed, sent to one group chat, forwarded to another, and by the time somebody needs it in review week it is buried under two hundred messages under three different file names.

This is the deliberate version. One address, one structure, and material that stays put after the semester ends. It is put together by students, which means it outlives the people who made it only if the next batch keeps adding to it. That is not a flaw in the plan, it is the plan.

Nothing here sits behind a login and nothing here costs money. If a student with a borrowed phone and a weak signal cannot open it, it does not belong on the shelf.

Grades 11 and 12

Four strands, one shelf.

The academy runs four Senior High School strands. Core subjects are shared across all of them, so most of the shelf is common ground and only the specialised half splits.

STEM

Science, Technology, Engineering and Mathematics

Advanced mathematics, laboratory science and the research track.

ABM

Accounting cycles, business mathematics, marketing and management.

HUMSS

Humanities and Social Sciences

Literature, philosophy, politics and the social sciences.

ICT

Information and Communications Technology

Computer systems servicing, programming and digital media.

Six kinds of material

What goes on the shelf.

Six kinds of thing and nothing else. The limit is deliberate, because a folder that accepts anything turns back into the group chat.

Handouts
The paper your teacher actually gave out, scanned once, named properly, and filed where the whole section can find it.
Slides
The deck from the discussion, for the days when the photo of the board came out at an angle and half the equation is gone.
Video guides
The explanation that finally made it click, kept so the next person spends ten minutes finding it instead of an evening.
References
Open textbooks and primary sources. Free to open, stable enough to cite, and no paywall waiting three clicks in.
Reviewers
Summaries written by students who already sat the test, credited to whoever wrote them, corrected by whoever finds the mistake.
Practice
Problem sets and drills, with the room to get them wrong somewhere that is not the actual long test.

A note on the links. Most of them open only with your Golden Faith Academy account, because that is how the teacher shared the file in the first place. The hub points at material where it already lives rather than keeping copies, so access stays exactly where the school set it. See the notice for the rest, or open Semester 1 to start reading.

MIT licensed

Public on purpose.

A hub only one person can edit dies the year that person graduates.

The whole thing is public. Any school is free to take it, put their own name on the front and run their own copy, and any student here can fix a broken link without waiting for permission from an account holder who has already left.

That is not generosity, it is maintenance. Handing out the keys is the only version of this that is still standing in three years.

Policy and takedown

Notice, policy and takedown.

What this is, what it is not, whose rules it runs under, and how to get something removed.

Independent project

Not an official school site.

This is a student-built project, made and published as portfolio work. It is not operated by Golden Faith Academy, it is not endorsed by or affiliated with the administration or the faculty, and it speaks for nobody but the students who put it together. The school name, crest and strand names appear here to describe who the material is for, and for no other reason.

The official Golden Faith Academy website is at gfa.edu.ph. Anything about admissions, tuition, requirements or school policy belongs there, not here.

School rules come first

We follow the academy, without exception.

This site operates under Golden Faith Academy's rules. Anything the administration or a teacher asks us to change, credit differently, restrict or take down is changed, credited, restricted or taken down. We do not ask for a reason and we do not argue the point.

Where a school policy and anything on this site ever conflict, the school policy wins. That is not a courtesy, it is the condition we built this under.

Access

Some links need your school account.

Teachers post to Classroom and Drive with access limited to the Golden Faith Academy domain, and that limit is deliberate. The hub points at those files where they already live. It does not host copies, mirror them, or route around a sign-in.

If a link asks you to log in and your school account will not open it, the material was not meant to be public, and we are not the ones who will make it so. Getting around that kind of restriction breaks school rules and is an offence under the Cybercrime Prevention Act of 2012 (Republic Act 10175). We do not go near it.

No answer keys

Study material only.

The hub links the things you study from: handouts, references, video lessons, reviewers and practice sets. It does not host answers to graded work, and it will not accept them.

Copying an answer is not studying, and a shelf that hands out answers is worth nothing to the person actually sitting the exam. Anything sent in that turns out to be a key to live coursework gets removed rather than filed.

Copyright and takedown

It stays the property of whoever made it.

Teachers keep the rights to their handouts, publishers to their books, and students to the reviewers they write. What the hub links is openly licensed, published free by its own author, or shared for classroom use under the educational provisions of the Intellectual Property Code of the Philippines (Republic Act 8293). Credit goes to the source every time.

If you own something here and want it gone, email us. No form, no lawyer, no reason needed. Name the item and it comes down, and any discussion happens afterwards. Removal first.

Privacy and conduct

Nothing about you is collected.

No accounts, no analytics, no cookies, no trackers, no third party scripts. Nothing about a visitor is gathered, stored or sent anywhere, which is the simplest way to stay clear of the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act 10173): hold no data and none can be mishandled. Your light or dark preference is saved in your own browser and goes no further.

Nothing here may name, picture, rank or target any student, teacher or member of staff. That follows DepEd's Child Protection Policy (DepEd Order No. 40, s. 2012) and the Anti-Bullying Act of 2013 (Republic Act 10627), and it is also simply the line. Anything of that kind is removed on sight.

This is a plain statement of how the hub is run, not legal advice. If the academy's administration would rather any of it were worded differently, tell us and we will use their wording instead. Reach us at motosk32@gmail.com.
